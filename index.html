Welcome to Melila Grama Panchayat

Melila Grama Panchayat is a rural local self-government institution located in Kottarakkara Taluk of Kollam District, Kerala. It is situated in the southern part of Kerala and forms an important rural administrative region of the district.

The region is characterised by its natural greenery, settlements, agricultural activities, roads and local water resources. Agriculture and other traditional occupations have contributed to the livelihood of the people. In recent years, the area has also experienced changes in settlement patterns, transportation and infrastructure.

Melila Grama Panchayat plays an important role in local administration, rural development, public services and the welfare of the community. This website provides geographical information, photographs and an interactive WebGIS platform to explore the different features of Melila Grama Panchayat.

About Melila

Melila is located in the eastern part of Kottarakkara Taluk in Kollam District. It comes under Vettikkavala Block Panchayat. The administrative headquarters of Kollam District is situated to the west of the region.

Melila is connected with neighbouring settlements through a network of roads. The region has residential areas, agricultural lands, educational institutions, religious centres and other public facilities. The local administration supports infrastructure development and community welfare.

Physical and Human Geography

Location: Melila is situated in Kottarakkara Taluk, Kollam District, Kerala.

Relief and Topography: The landscape consists of gently undulating terrain and lowland areas typical of the midland region of Kerala.

Climate: Melila experiences a tropical monsoon climate with seasonal rainfall and warm temperatures.

Vegetation: The area has tropical vegetation, home gardens, agricultural landscapes and scattered tree cover.

Land Use: Residential settlements, agricultural lands, roads and public infrastructure form important land-use categories.

Transportation: Road networks connect Melila with Kottarakkara and neighbouring settlements.
